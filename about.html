About New Covenant Church Dunstable
Discover our journey of faith, our vision for the future, and how God has blessed us to expand His kingdom through discipleship.
Out Belief History
Church mission: As a church we are passionate about building Strong, Christ-centered community that reflect God's love, compassion and hope. By integrating deeply into the fabric of our community, we aim to meet both spiritual and practical needs, fostering environment where people can experience the true love and transformative power of Jesus Christ. Church vision: At New Covenant Church Dunstable, we are a family friendly fellowship. We believe that the message of Christ has the power to transform lives, and through our mission, we seek to bring this life-changing truth to people in our community.

Our Vision

Discipleship

Making disciples who make disciples, following the Great Commission to spread the Gospel to all nations.

Community Impact

Building strong communities through service, outreach, and demonstrating Christ's love in practical ways.
